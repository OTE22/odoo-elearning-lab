# Editorial review of the Arabic courses

The three manuscripts are draft revision 1. Each uses the existing Arabic workshop as its starting point. The earlier material already contains workplace decisions, worked answers, and quizzes. The revision keeps the central cases and adds independent transfer problems, written justifications and a final task with an analytic marking guide.

| Manuscript | Teaching gap addressed | Evidence to inspect during review | Estimated study time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Cultural proposals](proposal_writing.ar.md) | The learner could reproduce the worked denominator or budget without recognizing which decision the calculation supports. | Lesson 3 changes the population and missing observations. The final library case requires a funding decision using new costs and payment dates. | 3 h 45 min |
| [AI-assisted cultural editing](ai_cultural_work.ar.md) | Checking names and dates alone misses errors in the relationship between a date and an event or between a verb and a visitor's action. | The learner distinguishes a loan date from a production date, repairs an instruction in English, and authorizes a bilingual release against a corrected source. | 3 h 45 min |
| [Digital safety and recovery](digital_safety.ar.md) | A successful restore can mask excessive access or a booking discrepancy. | The learner calculates both recovery measures, reconciles repeated identifiers, tests a staff role and explains the operational release decision. | 3 h 10 min |

Time budgets include two ten-minute module checks and a 45-minute independent project. They are author estimates awaiting a learner pilot. Institution names, case facts, grant conditions, payment dates, access roles and recovery targets are instructional assumptions. The reading at the end of each manuscript identifies the source of the underlying professional terminology or risk concept.

## Lesson-level editorial decisions

Use the manuscript filename, revision and numbered lesson as the review identifier. For example, the third proposal-writing lesson in revision 1 is `proposal_writing.ar.md / 1 / lesson 3`. A reviewer can mark a single lesson approved, request a change to the explanation or answer key, or return the associated final criterion for amendment. Record the specific passage, reason, reviewer and date. The module check and final project have their own headings for the same purpose.

All lessons, module checks and final projects currently await editorial approval.
